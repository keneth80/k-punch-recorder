K Punch Recorder Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

K Punch Recorder is designed to keep recordings under the user's control.

Information We Process

K Punch Recorder can process the following information on the user's device:

  • Audio recordings created by the user
  • Recording file names entered by the user
  • Transcript text generated from recordings
  • App settings such as language and editing preferences

Audio Recording

K Punch Recorder uses microphone access only when the user starts recording. Recordings are stored in the app's local storage unless the user chooses to share or export them.

Speech Recognition

When the user chooses text conversion, K Punch Recorder uses Apple's speech recognition features to create transcript segments. Speech recognition behavior may be subject to Apple's system services and privacy terms.

Sharing

When the user chooses to share a recording, K Punch Recorder exports the selected audio as an m4a file and hands it to the system share sheet. The destination is selected by the user.

Data Collection

K Punch Recorder does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics in this version.

The app does not intentionally transmit recordings, transcripts, or file names to a developer-operated server.

Data Retention

Recordings and transcripts remain in app storage until the user deletes them or removes the app. Shared files are handled by the destination selected by the user.

Permissions

K Punch Recorder may request:

  • Microphone access: to record audio
  • Speech recognition access: to convert recordings into text

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact the developer at https://github.com/keneth80.
